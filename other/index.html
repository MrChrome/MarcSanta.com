Other stuff

  • Michigan Interstates by Year Opened
    Interactive map of every Interstate segment in Michigan, colored by the year it opened.
  • Propaganda by iNF
    A browser port of iNF's 2001 PC demo, 3rd place at Mekka & Symposium 2001. Five minutes of real-time 3D by Kravitz and 2D artwork by tmk, cut to a Cornelius track like a music video. Turn the sound up; needs WebGL 2.
  • Master Yi: The Wuju Bladesman
    A League of Legends–themed arcade game at wujustyle.com. Play as Master Yi against waves of Teemos, using Alpha Strike, Meditate and Highlander, dodging blinding darts and mushroom traps, with a boss every fourth wave and a global high-score board. Works on desktop and phones.
