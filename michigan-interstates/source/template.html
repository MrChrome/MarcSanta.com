How years are assigned. Each segment shows the year it first opened to traffic as a full freeway, even if it was signed US 12, US 16, US 23, US 27, M-21, M-76 or M-78 at the time and became an Interstate later. Where one roadway opened before the other, the note says so. Segments marked ≈ have no exact date in the sources and use the best-supported year.
Sources. Opening dates from Christopher Bessert's Michigan Highways route histories (I-75, I-94, I-96, I-69, I-275) and Wikipedia's route articles for I-196, I-696, I-475, I-675, I-496, I-194, I-296 and I-375. Highway lines from Natural Earth's North America roads layer, split at interchange coordinates, so segment breaks are accurate to roughly a mile. I-296 is drawn schematically.
